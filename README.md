# Software Mixer downloads

Installers for **Ricks Mixer**, the multi-microphone recorder for depositions,
hearings and meetings: every voice on its own track, plus the full mix.

**Download and pricing:** https://www.softwaremixer.com

The latest installer is always on the [Releases](../../releases/latest) page.

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- Free version: 2 channels, records 1 channel plus the full mix
- Pro ($9.99/month): 6 channels, records every channel

Recordings never leave your computer.

Support: support@softwaremixer.com
